Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what data the ScrubBots mobile game ("the game", "we") uses on Android and iOS. ScrubBots is published by the developer listed on its Google Play and App Store pages. Contact: sekiphayit1982@gmail.com.

Summary

  • There is no account and no login. We never ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • We run no server of our own. Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier, with your consent where the law requires it.

1. Data stored on your device

The game saves your level progress, currencies, boosters, robots, cards, rewards, settings (sound, language, color symbols and similar) and the record of what you have bought on your device. This data never leaves your device through us. Uninstalling the game deletes it. It is not backed up to any server of ours; your device's own backup feature may include it, under Google's or Apple's terms.

The game has an optional "Share analytics" switch in Settings > Privacy. It is off unless you turn it on. In this version, gameplay statistics stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Reminder notifications, if you allow them, are scheduled on your device and contain no personal information.

2. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (Scrub Bucks packs and Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play Billing on Android and by Apple's App Store (StoreKit) on iOS. Your payment details are handled only by Google or Apple; we never see your card or account details. The game receives only the purchase confirmation it needs to deliver the item and to restore Remove Ads. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner ads, ads between levels and optional rewarded videos through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • the device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt),
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (views, taps) and diagnostic data such as crash logs from the ads software.

Consent. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalised. If you do not consent, ads may still be shown but are not personalised. On iOS the game asks for tracking permission with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, the IDFA is not shared.

Your choices. You can change your consent from the game's privacy options where offered, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" on iOS, or buy Remove Ads (rewarded videos stay available if you choose to watch them). Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Children

ScrubBots is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with Google (AdMob, Google Play) and Apple as described above, so that ads and purchases work.

6. Security and retention

Game data on your device stays until you uninstall the game. Data collected by Google or Apple is kept under their own policies. Their connections are encrypted in transit.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law, KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data on any server, you can delete all game data by uninstalling the game. For data held by Google or Apple, use their tools, or contact us and we will help.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
