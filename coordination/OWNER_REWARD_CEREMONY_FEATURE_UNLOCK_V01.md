Scope: reward reveals, pack opening, robot/collection/world unlock, feature pacing.

## 1. Reward presentation principle

Authoritative grants happen through game services. Presentation celebrates committed truth; it never becomes the authority that grants the reward.

Reopening/skipping/fast-forwarding a ceremony cannot duplicate grants.

## 2. Required ceremonies

- first-clear Results reward reveal;
- Gift Meter milestone;
- Daily reward;
- Tasks 3/3 completion;
- Standard Card Pack opening;
- Premium Card Pack opening;
- first-new-card / duplicate distinction;
- Collection set 9/9 completion;
- Master Collection completion;
- Robot unlock;
- feature unlock;
- world unlock/transition.

## 3. Pack opening

Standard Pack:
- 3 draws.

Premium Pack:
- 5 draws;
- at least one Rare-or-better according to authoritative pack logic.

Presentation shows new/duplicate and rarity truth. It cannot reroll contents.

## 4. Robot unlock

Robot unlock uses the canonical roster/assets and shows:
- robot identity;
- personality/role;
- canonical meta perk;
- Bot Part carryover.

The player may equip the new robot or keep the current robot.

## 5. Feature unlock pacing

FTUE/feature unlock order is data-driven.
Exact campaign unlock level numbers remain owner-tuning-required and must not be invented before approval.

A feature unlock ceremony:
- appears once per feature/version;
- can mark the relevant destination NEW until first visit;
- does not mint rewards unless separately authorized.

## 6. World progression

Future world ranges/unlock conditions require owner approval.
No hardcoded 1-100/101-200 assumptions.
World unlock presentation consumes a data-driven world registry after it exists.

## 7. Reduced Effects

Every celebration has a concise Reduced Effects path preserving all textual/reward truth.
