# SB-M35-005 — Dimensions

Status: IMPLEMENTED
Requirement: expose validated width/height and prove rectangular support plus
the 20..59 production envelope.

## Production owner
`LevelCatalogEntry.width` / `height` are copied from the `LevelData` that
already passed `LevelValidator` (structural) and `ProductionLevelValidator`
(band). Cell count is always derived as `width * height` in `LevelData`
(never a separately-trusted stored field, per ADR-008).

## Direct tests
- `_production_catalog_loads` — M21 20x20 exposed.
- `_rectangular_boundary` — synthetic EASY 24x28 accepted, rectangular dims
  surfaced verbatim.

## Failure condition
Width or height not exposed, rectangular level rejected, or cross-band dim
accepted through catalog.

## Actual
PASS.
