## How to rate

Play each level once from a fresh attempt at 1x speed, without boosters, then fill its row.

- **Perceived difficulty** 1..7: 1 = trivial, 4 = noticeable thought, 7 = very demanding.
- **Fairness** 1..7: 1 = felt unfair or opaque, 7 = every obstacle was readable and fair.
- **Boredom / engagement** 1..7: 1 = boring or repetitive, 7 = fully engaged.
- **Attempt duration**: optional, in minutes and seconds (mm:ss).
- **Intended class feel**: compared with the class label in the table, did the level feel *easier*, *about right* or *harder*?
- **Completed first attempt?**: yes/no (optional).
- **Notes**: optional free text.

Rate from your own play only. Leave a cell empty if you did not observe it. Do not estimate for other players.
