Floor-tone note: tones are capped at about 0.052 relative luminance. The spec's ">= 1.3:1 against every C01..C16" rule only allows the band 0.015..0.053, because C14 Dark Gray (0.084) and C08 Dark Blue (0.088) sit just above it. So every candidate is the rail-glow hue at the brightest legal level: deep halo, mid glow, cyan core and deep blue, about 2.8x BG01. The baked navy board field (0.042) is lifted to tone 0. The difference is visible but modest; a much brighter floor would need the contrast rule relaxed. The owner picks the tone from the side-by-side capture.

Files outside the spec's diff list: `home_style.gd`, `home_screen.gd`, `main.gd`, `production_gameplay_host.gd`. They are needed for "reuse the same component and the same No Ads rule" without duplicating the numbers.
