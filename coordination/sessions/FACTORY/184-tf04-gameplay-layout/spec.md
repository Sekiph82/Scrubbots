## Assumptions
- "Board floor" = the dark navy board backdrop (BoardBackground under CLEARED cells plus the baked navy interior), because that is the only board-area "floor" and the rail glow is the neighbouring bright element. The owner request overrides the use of BG01 as the on-screen backdrop; the palette BG01 constant is left intact.
- Scrubby, props and bubble are baked in the masters, so the change requires regenerating the six master PNGs (SHA lock updated); a runtime overlay was rejected because cropped overlays would leave a visible pavement patch and the original beneath.
- Exact new rects (booster band, anchors) are my layout proposal fitted to the free space (Supply bottom ~1503, banner worst case top ~1685 at 720x1280); builder may shift any anchor by up to 8 ref px only if requirement 1.d/1.e relations still hold, and must then update the numbers in the test and docs consistently.
- Banner overlays the bottom of the shell (as the old `Shell.AD` band did) rather than shrinking the shell, so board size is unchanged; booster band was chosen to clear the tallest banner (112 px) at every matrix viewport.
- Booster row is right of Scrubby (x 332..840, not centred) because Scrubby grows 25% and the sign moves up beside Supply; this is the smallest change that keeps everything unobscured.
