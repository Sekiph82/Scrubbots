2. **real_play_levels L30, m55_long_session L30 and m46 density: real-play timing follows the slot art, as designed.**
   - The cause: `SlotOriginProvider.origin_for_slot` maps the visible slot top-centre (`FiveSlotStrip.get_slot_anchor_global`) into board cells. Relative to the rail, the gap is 0.161 of the rail height against 0.169 before; for 59x59 the slot-to-bottom-rail leg is about 10.3 cells against 11.3 before. Horizontal lanes are within 0.005 of the rail width.
   - The effect: dispatch timing shifts slightly. 29 of 30 campaign plans still win unchanged (`real_play_verify --levels all` before the fix). L30's order deadlocks: before the fix it was LOST, stuck at 68/120 clicks with 730 cells left; it won in 26037 frames on the v02 shells. The p13 density peak goes from 92 (previous green run) to 85; the floor of 90 was set at RM-001 when the peak was 114.
   - Why I did not decouple the timing from the art: keeping the old timing would mean routing bots from invisible v02 slot points. That breaks the audited M29 contract and every suite that pins origin == visible anchor (m29_exact_slot_origin_evidence, m29_presentation_identity_evidence, m28 static shell, c004 anchors, ...). The new numbers are the honest consequence of owner-approved art.
   - **Fix:** `tools/real_play_verify.gd -- --levels 30 --regen`, the same established path L18/L20/L22/L23/L25/L30 already went through. It found an order in `SEARCH L30 level_030_clown SOLVED nodes=5/400 canonical-win=true`, and the result is `LEVEL L30 ... canonical-1/60=WON frames=24664`. It reorders L30's `intendedColumnClicks` only: the same 120 clicks, as a text splice, plus the provenance record. Every other level is untouched and still wins with its unchanged plan.

   - Slot pop hold, refill-at-once and every other rule are untouched. No gameplay code changed.
