# SB-M38-011 — No duplicate grant

Status: IMPLEMENTED
Requirement: stable transaction IDs make repeated/reentrant callbacks
idempotent. Duplicate first-clear or WON cannot double-grant SB, Bot Part, or
Gift Meter progress.

## Production owner
- `WinStreakService` uses per-level tx ids: `streak_sb:L<n>`, `streak_bp5:L<n>`,
  and the gift feed `gift:L<n>`.
- `RewardGrantService.grant` is a no-op on a known tx id.
- `GiftMeterService.add_streak_sb` is a no-op on a known tx id.
- `_processed_levels` blocks re-processing at the streak layer (defense in depth).

## Direct tests
`_duplicate_and_reentrant` — duplicate + 10 reentrant calls grant SB once,
streak stays 1. `_reward_service_idempotent` — duplicate tx no-op.
