# Standard Pack — Owner Review (SB-M43-064 V02)

This scene is the **exact shipping V02 Standard Pack ceremony**, wrapped in review-only tooling so you can judge the live motion.

## Steps

1. Open the Godot project: `C:\Users\sekip\Desktop\ScrubBots\project.godot`
2. In the **FileSystem** panel, open: `res://tests/tools/owner_review/standard_pack_owner_review.tscn`
3. Press **F6 — Run Current Scene**. (F5 is not required.)
4. Look at the first screen: the Standard Pack alone, "Tap to open". It waits for you.
5. **Click once** and watch the full opening (frames 01 → 09, the 3 cards coming out, the pack disappearing).
6. Wait for the three cards with **Collection** (upper-left) and **Cards Exchange** (upper-right) and "Tap to collect your cards".
7. **Click again** and watch the routing: NEW cards fly to Collection, DUPLICATE cards fly to Cards Exchange.
8. Press **R** to replay from the closed pack.
9. Press **E** to switch between Full and **Reduced Effects** (it restarts from the closed pack). Press E again to go back.
10. Press **1 / 2 / 3** to change the cards (each restarts from the closed pack):
    - **1** = mixed: Mud Blob NEW · Greasy Pan DUPLICATE · Scrubbot Prime NEW (default)
    - **2** = all NEW: Scrubby · Turbo Wheels · Storm Cleaner
    - **3** = repeated duplicate: Mighty Mop NEW · Mighty Mop DUPLICATE · Sludge Beast DUPLICATE

After the cards are routed a small grey "Review complete" note appears; press R / E / 1 / 2 / 3 to continue reviewing. Close the window to stop.

## Good to know

- Nothing here changes your save, Scrub Bucks, Collection or Cards Exchange. The cards are fixed test results.
- Nothing is clicked for you: both clicks are yours.
- Only give **OWNER VISUAL PASS** after judging the live motion (timing, transitions, composition, feel).
