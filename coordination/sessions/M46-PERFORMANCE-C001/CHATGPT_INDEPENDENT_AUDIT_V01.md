## Independent source findings
1. **Meaningful production-host coverage (PASS — source architecture):** p16 builds a Level Data v2 fixture through `LevelLoader` and `ProductionGameplayHost`, in a real-sized 1080×2160 `SubViewport`. It asserts that the execution slot origin falls below the board and advances the real runtime/production input via `activate_front`; it is not merely a mock solver.
2. **Fixture validity (PASS — static):** A 59×59 board with a 2-cell VOID frame leaves 55×55 = **3,025 artwork cells** and **456 VOID cells**. It has nine stripe colours plus three pocket colours, **12 canonical IDs**. Three separate 9×9 pocket regions contain concentric C05/C09/C16 layers with a deeper core. `_pocket_plan()` counts non-VOID cells, splits into batches of at most 30 and sends pocket batches through Supply column 4; stripes occupy the other four columns. The test's source asserts WON and 3,025 cleared artwork cells.
3. **WAITING + unlock checks (PASS WITH ASSERTION GAP):** p16 counts occupied WAITING slots per frame, tests that waiting starts by frame five and accumulates at least 60 frames, and asserts P1 first-clear < P2 first-clear < P3 first-clear. The builder log reports **peak 5 WAITING slots** and unlock frames **1,920 < 19,120 < 19,630**. However, **peak_waiting == 5 is printed but not asserted**; first-clear timing does not prove all later cells respect a complete formal unlock rule. Follow-up: add an explicit `peak_waiting >= 5` gate if this synthetic fixture reliably requires five, and assert waiting actually clears/recovery occurs before WON. Distinguish these from the already genuinely asserted weaker waiting criterion.
4. **Pause/speed (PASS WITH COVERAGE GAP):** during p16 the runner ticks 60 paused frames and asserts the board state did not change. It calls speed 1× then 2× and reaches WON, but **does not assert the effective speed state/transition**, so do not claim exact speed transitions as independently proven.
5. **CPU budget and device distinction (PLAUSIBLE; BUILDER_LOCAL_PASS ONLY):** Claude reports complete p16 WON after **20,828 frames**, mean **2.10 ms**, p99 **9.52 ms**, max **45.7 ms**, up to **117 agents**; the suite gates `mean <= 4` and `p99 <= 16.7`. These are Godot 4.7.2 **headless laptop CPU** figures. They exclude mobile GPU/render and include an explicitly changed zero-headless-idle-sleep setting. They are *not* 60fps on phones, and max frames/target-selection bursts are not gated by p99. The M46 log reports target selection spikes up to 24.2 ms and 2,071 full reachability-mask builds; this suggests where to profile first on a real slow device, not an unapproved algorithm rewrite.
