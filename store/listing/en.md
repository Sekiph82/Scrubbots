## Full description (4000)
```
Pixel art is everywhere and it is up to the Scrubbots to clean it all away!

ScrubBots is a colorful puzzle game about cute cleaning robots. Every level is a pixel-art picture. Tap a batch of robots, they take one of your five slots, and each Scrubbot rolls out to a pixel of its own color, scrubs it away and disappears. Clear every pixel to win the level.

It sounds simple, but robots can only reach pixels that are open. Pixels hidden behind other colors have to wait until the way is clear, so the order you choose matters. Plan ahead, keep your five slots moving and watch the whole picture disappear.

FEATURES
- 200 hand-made pixel-art levels: animals, robots, monsters, heroes and more
- Easy to learn, with a smooth mix of relaxed levels and tricky ones
- Five slots, many colors: choose the right batch at the right time
- 4 boosters for tough spots: +1 Slot, Random, Selector and Tornado
- 10 robot helpers to unlock, each with its own bonus
- Collect 135 cards in 15 sets, open card packs and trade spares in the Cards Exchange
- Daily rewards, daily tasks, the Gift Meter and win-streak rewards
- 2x speed boosts when you want to go faster
- Optional color symbols for color-blind players
- Play in English or Turkish
- No account or sign-up needed: your progress is saved on your device

ScrubBots is free to play. It shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases (Scrub Bucks packs, and Remove Ads, which turns off banner and between-level ads). Rewarded videos are always optional. You can play the whole game without paying.

Grab your brush, the Scrubbots are ready to roll!
```
